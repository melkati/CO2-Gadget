Low Power

Low power mode puts the device to deep sleep between measurements. This reduces battery drain significantly but disables real-time connectivity while sleeping.

Low Power Options
Select if you want to use low power mode. This will reduce the power consumption of the device. Use with caution as some features will be disabled.
Time to wait before entering low power mode. This will give you time to interact with the device before it enters low power mode.
Time to be in low power mode after a measurement. This will reduce the power consumption of the device. Use with caution as some features will be disabled.
Connect to WiFi every X cycles. This will reduce the power consumption of the device. Use with caution as some features will be disabled.
Redraw the display every X cycles. This will reduce the power consumption of the device. Use with caution as some features will be disabled.
Activate BLE on wakeup. This will allow CO2 Gadget to send data to your phone or other BLE devices.
Activate WiFi on wakeup. This will allow CO2 Gadget to send data to your network (this is needed for MQTT).
Send data via MQTT on wakeup. This will allow CO2 Gadget to send data to your network (this is needed for Home Assistant).
Send data via ESPNow on wakeup. This will allow CO2 Gadget to send data to other ESP32 devices.
Turn on the display for a moment when the device wakes up. This will allow you to see the current readings.
Threshold Options

These are the thresholds for the display when in low power mode.

General Settings
Apply the threshold only when the device is in low power mode. Apply the threshold when the device is not in low power mode.
Thresholds Options for Display
The absolute value difference of CO2 in ppm that will trigger the threshold. The display will not be updated until the threshold is met.
The percentage difference of CO2 in ppm that will trigger the threshold. The display will not be updated until the threshold is met.
Both conditions must be met to trigger the threshold. At least one condition must be met to trigger the threshold.
The absolute value difference of temperature in °C that will trigger the threshold. The display will not be updated until the threshold is met.
The percentage difference of temperature in °C that will trigger the threshold. The display will not be updated until the threshold is met.
Both conditions must be met to trigger the threshold. At least one condition must be met to trigger the threshold.
The absolute value difference of humidity in %rH that will trigger the threshold. The display will not be updated until the threshold is met.
The percentage difference of humidity in %rH that will trigger the threshold. The display will not be updated until the threshold is met.
Both conditions must be met to trigger the threshold. At least one condition must be met to trigger the threshold.
