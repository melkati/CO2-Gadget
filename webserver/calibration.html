Forced (Manual) Calibration

Warning: Forced calibration resets the sensor baseline. Only use this in a known-CO₂ environment (e.g. outdoors at ~420 ppm, or using a calibration gas). An incorrect value will cause permanently wrong readings until the next calibration.

Set the CO₂ concentration (ppm) to calibrate against. Typical outdoor air is 400–420 ppm. Valid range: 400–2000 ppm.

About Automatic Baseline Correction (ABC)

Most CO₂ sensors (SCD30, SCD40/41, MH-Z19…) include an automatic baseline correction (ABC) algorithm. ABC periodically re-calibrates the sensor assuming that the lowest reading in a given period corresponds to fresh outdoor air (~400 ppm). This works well in spaces that are regularly ventilated but may drift in permanently elevated CO₂ environments.

You can enable or disable ABC in Preferences → Advanced.
